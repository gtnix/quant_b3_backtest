# Trading Strategies Types - Research Notes

## Source: Investopedia - Active Trading Strategies

### 1. Scalping
- **Timeframe**: Seconds to minutes
- **Holding Period**: Very short (seconds to minutes)
- **Goal**: Profit from small price movements
- **Characteristics**:
  - High frequency trading
  - Quick decision-making required
  - High transaction costs due to frequency
  - Requires discipline and focus
  - Small profit per trade, many trades
  - Minimal overnight risk
- **Key Considerations**: Transaction fees, bid-ask spreads

### 2. Day Trading
- **Timeframe**: Intraday (within single trading day)
- **Holding Period**: Minutes to hours (closed by market close)
- **Goal**: Profit from intraday price movements
- **Characteristics**:
  - All positions closed by end of day
  - No overnight risk
  - High potential profits per trade
  - Flexible work location
  - Fast-paced environment
  - Used by institutions and individuals
- **Key Considerations**: High risk, emotional trading decisions, transaction costs

### 3. Swing Trading
- **Timeframe**: Days to months
- **Holding Period**: Few days to few months
- **Goal**: Capture short to intermediate-term price movements
- **Characteristics**:
  - Buy low, sell high on short-term trends
  - More time for analysis than day trading
  - Lower transaction costs than scalping/day trading
  - Requires technical analysis
  - Exposed to overnight and weekend risk
  - More flexible than day trading
- **Key Considerations**: Market trend analysis, risk management, discipline

### 4. Position Trading
- **Timeframe**: Months to years
- **Holding Period**: Several months to years/decades
- **Goal**: Profit from major long-term trends
- **Characteristics**:
  - Less active than other strategies
  - Uses fundamental and technical analysis
  - Lower transaction costs
  - Higher potential long-term gains
  - More time for decision-making
  - Limited liquidity
- **Key Considerations**: Long-term market exposure, opportunity cost for short-term moves

## Key Insights for TPM Module

### Timeframe Classification:
- **Ultra-short**: Scalping (seconds-minutes) - NOT APPLICABLE (we focus on daily data)
- **Short-term**: Day Trading (hours) - LIMITED (max 1h bars)
- **Short to Medium**: Swing Trading (days-weeks) - PRIMARY FOCUS
- **Medium to Long**: Position Trading (months-years) - SECONDARY FOCUS

### Parameters to Consider:
1. Holding period (days/weeks/months)
2. Transaction costs sensitivity
3. Risk exposure (overnight, weekend, long-term)
4. Analysis type (technical vs fundamental)
5. Trading frequency
6. Profit target per trade
7. Time commitment required
8. Liquidity requirements
